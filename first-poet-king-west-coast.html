The West Coast’s First Poet King: A Legacy Built on Ink and Iron

King Kavi Alupendra, a staunch devotee of Tuluveshwari and Mukambika, stands as the Aadhi Kavi—the First Poet—and the first Poet King of the entire West Coast. His vision elevated Tulu to become the first literary language of our coastal civilization.

The Significance of Devi Mahatme

A crowning achievement of his era was the formal recording of the Devi Mahatme. Tulu holds the distinction of being the first Dravidian language to formally record this significant work, proving its sophistication as a literary vehicle.
