The Aadhi Kavi: The First Poet King of the West Coast

Kavi Alupendra is recognized as the Aadhi Kavi—the First Poet—and the inaugural Poet King of the West Coast. He architected a cultural paradigm, formalizing Tulu as the language of the court and transitioning the region from an oral tradition to a recorded literary powerhouse.

The West Coast’s First Epic: Devi Mahatme

The Devi Mahatme stands as the West Coast's oldest available literary work in Tulu. It holds the monumental distinction of being the first Dravidian language to formally record this epic, serving as irrefutable proof of Tulu's early status as a high-culture literary vehicle.

Critical Attribution: The Significance of the 'Kavi/Kabi' Title

Alupendra’s title of Kavi was a credential signifying a monarch who mastered both statecraft and the aesthetic arts. His courtly scholars navigated complex metres and phonological demands, ensuring that Tulu composition surpassed the rigorous benchmarks codified in works like Kavirajamarga.

The Digital Reconstruction: Recovering the Script

The Tuluva Guardian is spearheading the development of an independent Android keyboard for the Tulu-Tigalari script. By integrating native font support, we are breaking the digital barriers that have kept our literature inaccessible, ensuring the script remains a functional part of our identity.

Fulfilling the Criteria for Classical Language Status

Tulu’s claim to classical status is not merely cultural; it is structural. By providing evidence of high antiquity, a recorded literary tradition independent of modern influence, and the authorship of the Devi Mahatme as the first Dravidian epic of its kind, Tulu satisfies the essential criteria for classical recognition. Our research demonstrates that Tulu was a fully realized literary language, possessing its own unique script and rigorous poetic standards long before the present era. This historical reality demands that Tulu be formally classified as a classical language, preserving its rightful place in India's intellectual heritage.
