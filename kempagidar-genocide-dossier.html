TULUVA MASSACRE IN SOUTH INDIA

A Century of Erasure, A Biological Genocide, A Nation Awakening

I. The Linguistic Death Sentence

The denial of official status for the Tulu language is not a mere bureaucratic oversight; it is a structural weapon of exclusion that has historically acted as a death sentence for the Tuluva nation. For over a century, the administrative machinery has functioned in tongues foreign to our soil, creating a lethal disconnect between the state and the people it claims to serve.

In the rural heartlands of the cashew belt, where Tulu is the only bridge to reality, thousands were left defenseless against a healthcare system that refused to speak their language. The state effectively performed a linguistic lobotomy on its citizens, rendering them invisible in the eyes of the law and the clinic.

II. The Biological Frontier: The 30,000 Souls

The investigation now formally documents a staggering toll: an estimated 30,000 Tuluvas sacrificed on the altar of state expansionism and corporate greed. This figure represents the cumulative total of a century of systematic destruction, peaking with the "Biological Genocide" of the Endosulfan era between 1980 and 2001.

For twenty years, state-owned corporations launched aerial sorties over the lush plantations... raining down poison with total disregard for the biological integrity of the Tuluva nation. Every life cut short by the "Yellow Rain" is a testament to the state’s criminal intent.

III. The Kasaragod Betrayal and Medical Exile

The massacre was perfected through the systematic denial of medical sovereignty in the Tulu-speaking borderlands of Kasaragod. By intentionally hollowing out the local healthcare infrastructure, the administrative machinery ensured that the victims of the chemical war had no choice but to enter a state of "Medical Exile."

The 2020 border blockades were merely the modern, visible manifestation of a siege that has been operational for over four decades, proving that to the state, a Tuluva life is only as valuable as the land it can be extracted from.

IV. The New Front: A Writ of Mandamus

Today, the Tuluva Guardian declares that the era of passive suffering and the "60-Year Blackout" has officially ended; we are moving from the hospital wards and the cashew groves to the high courts of law.

Our demand for a Writ of Mandamus is not just a legal request; it is a battle cry for the restoration of a nation that has been systematically bled dry by an occupying administrative force. The blackout is shattered, the evidence is live on the Tuluva Guardian portal, and the nation is rising from the poisoned earth.
