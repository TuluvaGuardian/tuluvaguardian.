By Editorial Desk | Published: May 31, 2026
Administrative Review: High-level discussions behind closed doors focus heavily on expanding water diversion layouts across coastal river basins.

MANGALURU: Investigative reporting has confirmed that the state government is blueprinting a new strategy to expand the controversial Yettinahole project, focusing heavily on a proposal to divert additional water from the Kumaradhara River basin.

This development arrives as coastal communities and environmental analysts continue to raise deep structural concerns regarding the long-term ecological impact on downstream water availability and the sensitive ecosystems of the Western Ghats.
