The strategic delivery arrives precisely at a critical juncture, as the First Appellate Authority reviews the high-stakes transparency battle surrounding First Appeal No. SECKC/A/2026/60004.

By bypassing traditional administrative delays and sending these formal arguments directly to the legislative core, TGIK has placed the state's highest decision-makers on notice. The Legal Groundwork: Public Interest Over Corporate Exemption

The formal TGIK representation tears through months of bureaucratic silence with absolute statutory precision:

  • No Exemption Under Section 8: TGIK forcefully argues that the Gayatri Committee Report—the comprehensive study evaluating the parameters for the officialization of the Tulu language—is a matter of immense cultural, linguistic, and historical importance to millions of citizens. It fails to meet any statutory criteria for secrecy or exemption.
  • Public Funds Mandate Public Access: Because this critical report was compiled using public funds, TGIK asserts that a democratic framework strictly requires citizens to have unhindered access to the policy assessments and structural feasibility metrics regarding regional language recognition.
  • The Ultimate Relief Demanded: The prayer before the state authorities is absolute: direct the Public Information Officer (PIO) to immediately provide an unedited, complete copy of the Gayatri Committee Report originally demanded in RTI Application No. SECKC/R/2026/60032.
The formal institutional documentation has successfully landed on the record. The state framework must now directly answer the statutory parameters raised by TGIK.
