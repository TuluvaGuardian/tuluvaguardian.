TAULAVA GĒL: TGIK Knocks on Vidhana Sabha Doors to Demand Transparency

By Tuluva Guardian Desk | Published: July 1, 2026

BENGALURU: The institutional struggle for the official status of the Tulu language has scaled the highest walls of state governance. Yesterday, June 30, 2026, the Taulava Gēl Inaya Koolya (TGIK) transitioned public interest research into an aggressive institutional offensive, dispatching a comprehensive legislative representation directly to the floor of the Vidhana Sabha.

The strategic delivery arrives precisely at a critical juncture, as the First Appellate Authority reviews the high-stakes transparency battle surrounding First Appeal No. SECKC/A/2026/60004.

By bypassing traditional administrative delays and sending these formal arguments directly to the legislative core, TGIK has placed the state's highest decision-makers on notice. The campaign demands that the case be evaluated strictly on its unyielding legal merits under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

The Legal Groundwork: Public Interest Over Corporate Exemption

The formal TGIK representation tears through months of bureaucratic silence with absolute statutory precision:

  • No Exemption Under Section 8: TGIK forcefully argues that the Gayatri Committee Report—the comprehensive study evaluating the parameters for the officialization of the Tulu language—is a matter of immense cultural, linguistic, and historical importance.
  • Public Funds Mandate Public Access: Because this critical report was compiled using public funds, TGIK asserts that a democratic framework strictly requires citizens to have unhindered access.
  • The Ultimate Relief Demanded: The prayer before the state authorities is absolute: direct the Public Information Officer (PIO) to immediately provide an unedited, complete copy of the report.
The formal institutional documentation has successfully landed on the record. The state framework must now directly answer the statutory parameters raised by TGIK.

From Historical Foundations to Modern Accountability

This move marks a significant transition for the campaign, driving the core mission of Taulava Gēl straight into the halls of power. TGIK continues to track all legislative updates regarding the linguistic status and the preservation of the historic script.

The ultimate hearing has begun. The paper war has hit the record.
